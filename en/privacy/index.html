Bilana is an iPhone app that helps building and property managers track dues, utilities, installment payments and expenses. This page explains how the app handles the information you enter.

  • Bilana does not require an account.
  • Everything you enter is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or to any other server.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking tools are used.
  • Your information never reaches us, so it is never sold or shared.

Information you enter

Building names and addresses, units, residents' names and phone numbers, payments, expenses and message templates are kept in a local database on your device. Only you can access them. Deleting the app deletes this information from the device.

If iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) is turned on, app data may be included in Apple's backup according to your device settings. Those backups are subject to Apple's terms; we have no access to them.

Backup file

When you create a file with Help › Data Backup › Back Up, you choose where to save it or whom to share it with (Files, AirDrop, email, etc.). The file is not sent to us. It contains residents' names and phone numbers, so keep it somewhere safe.

Calls, SMS and WhatsApp

When you call a resident or send a reminder, Bilana opens the relevant app (Phone, Messages, WhatsApp) with the number and message. You send the message yourself, and it is subject to that app's terms.

PDF reports

Reports are created on your device and shared only if you choose to share them.

Permissions

Bilana does not access your contacts, location, camera or photos. The file picker is used only when restoring a backup, to open the file you select.

Residents' personal data

You enter residents' names and phone numbers yourself. It is your responsibility to inform residents about how this information is used and stored for building management; you are the data controller for it (e.g. under the GDPR). Bilana does not process this data; it only keeps it on your device.

In-app purchases

Bilana is currently free and contains no in-app purchases.

Children

Bilana is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page and the date above will be updated.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone. Bilana requires no account and never sends your information to a server. See the Privacy Policy for details.

I'm switching phones. What happens to my data?

On the old phone, create a backup file with Help › Data Backup › Back Up and move it to the new phone (AirDrop, Files, email). On the new phone, choose the file with Help › Data Backup › Restore. Restoring replaces the data currently on the new phone.

How do I change the language or currency?

Under Help › Preferences. Changing the currency does not convert amounts; only the symbol changes.

A unit has a new resident. What should I do?

Tap the unit in the Units list and update the name and phone number. The new resident appears from this month on; past months and their payments keep the previous resident's name.

Is Bilana free?

All features are currently free.
